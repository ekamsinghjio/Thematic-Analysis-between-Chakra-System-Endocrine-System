Sacral Chakra- Sacral Chakra or Svadhishthan Chakra is 
located below the navel. It is mostly in charge of sexual and creative functions. It 
elicits pleasure, desire, and happiness (Stelter, 2016). This Chakra is associated with 
reproduction, genitals (ovary and testes) and sexual hormones (Majumdar, 2018). 
Blockage of this Chakra causes poor sexual impulses, low mood, and a decrease in 
creativity. This Chakra is symbolized by an orange color (Hale, 2022).
