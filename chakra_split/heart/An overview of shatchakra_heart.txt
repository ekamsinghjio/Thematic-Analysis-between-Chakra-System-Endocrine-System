Anahata- Anahata, the fourth Chakra, is located at the level of the sternum's depression in the spinal 
column, behind the base of the heart. It regulates the heart's functioning and is analogous to the cardiac 
plexus of nerves.14 
 Visuddha- Vishuddha, the fifth Chakra, is located in the vertebral column at the level of the throat pit. This 
Chakra is associated with the cervical plexus of nerves and governs the thyroid complex, as well as the 
upper palate, the epiglottis, and certain articulatory systems.15 
Agjna- Ajna, the pineal gland, which is located in the brain's midline just above the spinal column, is the 
sixth Chakra. This Chakra regulates a man's onset of sexual activity and his muscles. According to Tantra 
and Yoga, all aspects of the disciple's life are completely under the command of the Ajna Chakra, or 
command center.16
